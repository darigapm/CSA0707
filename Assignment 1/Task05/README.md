# Task 5 – Wi-Fi 6 or 5G for Moving Equipment

## 1. Hand-over Calculations
- **Wi-Fi 6:** 1,200 m / 30 m = 40 hand-overs → 40 × 150 ms = 6,000 ms (6s outage)[cite: 2].
- **Private 5G:** 1,200 m / 300 m = 4 hand-overs → 4 × 20 ms = 80 ms total outage[cite: 2].

## 2. 5G Service Mapping
- **AGVs & Robot Controllers:** URLLC (Ultra-Reliable Low-Latency Communication)[cite: 2]
- **CCTV Streaming:** eMBB (Enhanced Mobile Broadband)[cite: 2]
- **Vibration Sensors:** mMTC (Massive Machine Type Communications)[cite: 2]
